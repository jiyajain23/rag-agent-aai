Q: ATS units
A: ATS units (ATSUs) provide air traffic services, including:

1. Flight information service
2. Alerting service
3. Air traffic advisory service
4. Air traffic control service (area control service, approach control service, or aerodrome control service)

Their responsibilities include:

a) Transmitting information pertinent to the safe conduct of the flight without expecting a reply from the aircraft.
b) Monitoring and plotting the progress of the flight, and coordinating transfer of control with adjacent ATS units without requiring transmissions or other responses from the aircraft.
c) Informing and keeping informed other ATS units, and forwarding information to the aerodrome operator.

ATS units should also:

- Transmit routine and special air-reports to the associated meteorological office as soon as practicable.
- Transmit special air-reports to other aircraft concerned, and to other ATS units concerned, as soon as practicable.
